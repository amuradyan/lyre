## On Lyre repo and workflow

A major rehaul has started. The repo is a monorepo now, split in two: the Lyre lang w\ engine and the Lyre course. The course will then require the lang as a dependency. This will allow for the separation of the Lyre development as a program from the Lyre course development. A step forward in lang will be documented as a series of slides, removing the course-internal Lyre implementation.
