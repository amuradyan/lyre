## On rewriting synth

Now that the moving inside the repo is done, let us get back to the slides. The Synth chapter is rushed. It is good until slide `Pluck` and from there it's just an AI raw material I was supposed to polish later. Time to do that.

Lately I have been going down the path of examining a sample of a real lyre pluck - an A#3. To analyze its waveform and spectrum a few widgets were drafted but the UX is not quite there. The sample analysis is really useful since it shows how complex a real pluck is and provides clear guidance of what pitch starts when and where and how the timbre evolves.

We will start at `Pluck` and explore our way through the sample, figure out what is needed and arrive to a diagram of the synth stages:

    Oscillators -> Mixer -> Filters -> ADSR Envelopes -> Filter Envelopes -> Output

From there we move on to implementing the pieces or a certain subset of them.
