## Rewriting the Synth chapter

The whole chapter needs redesign. We start at the very beginning, set the goal to sound as close to a lyre as possible, and, to achieve that, we start with analyzing a sample - an A#3 pluck from a lyre. We look at its waveform and spectrum, analyze it while learning about concepts, figure out the ways we can do that, compare to the blocks we already have and such.

In the end we should have a pretty useful scheme of the synth stages, and a plan for implementing them in Lyre. I suppose we'll need oscillators/shapes/, a mixer of a sort /dB incoming/, waveform overlay, a low-pass filter /resonance?/ with envelope and an ADSR envelope for amplitude shaping.

This will provide better grounds for justifying what and when we shall implement.
