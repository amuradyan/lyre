## On n-pole filters via patches

Another use case for patches /lambdas/ besides notch and band-pass: building n-pole low and high-pass filters. Our `lowpass` is first-order - 6 dB per octave, the mildest possible slope. Cascading it k times gives roughly 6k dB per octave, so a 4-pole Moog-ish filter is just four `lowpass` calls stacked.

A patch lets the user define `npole` once and call `(npole 4 cutoff source)` instead of hand-writing the nest each time. Even recursive patches become plausible - n-pole defined as lowpass around (n-1)-pole, bottoming out at n=1. Gives the language something real to chew on without touching the core primitives.
