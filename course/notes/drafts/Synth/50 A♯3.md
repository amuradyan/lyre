Below is how an A#3 plucked on a lyre sounds and looks like. You can see frequencies the sound is comprised of on the horizontal axis and their loudness on vertical axis, measured in dB - decibels.

![spectrum-analyzer](course/public/lyre-As3.wav)

dB is a unit for measuring sound amplitude. The values are negative because we're measuring relative to the maximum possible digital level /called dBFS - Full Scale/. Think of it like measuring depth below sea level - 0dB is the surface /loudest possible/, and everything else is below that.

The louder a frequency, the closer to 0dB. The quieter, the more negative. In our pluck, the fundamental frequency A♯3 peaks around -25dB, while quieter harmonics sit below -35dB or even -50dB.
