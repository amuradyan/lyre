# Long time, no acronyms
<!-- slide-id: d1999f6a-103c-4200-8b2b-34220899fbb7 -->

__ADSR__ /Attack, Decay, Sustain, Release/ is a standard way to shape sound amplitude over time.

* __Attack__ - how quickly the sound reaches full volume /fade-in/
* __Decay__ - how quickly it drops to sustained level
* __Sustain__ - the steady volume level maintained while the note plays
* __Release__ - how quickly the sound fades to silence /fade-out/

![ADSR envelope diagram](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ea/ADSR_parameter.svg/640px-ADSR_parameter.svg.png)

Different instruments have different ADSR characteristics. _Piano_ has fast attack, medium decay, low sustain and medium release, while _lyre_ has fast attack, quick decay, medium sustain, fast release. Plucked strings behave similarly.

Our fade-in\fade-out is a simplified ADSR with just _attack_ and _release_. Let's add the _decay_, which is the pitch but at a smaller amplitude.
