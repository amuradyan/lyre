# On to interpreting

We're not quite done with the tokenizer - there are edge cases and refinements ahead - but we're pretty much there for now.

Here's where we stand: we have a JS engine that can play sound, and we have a tokenizer that can turn Lyre expressions into something that can be interpreted. What we don't have is the interpreter itself.

Let's build one.
