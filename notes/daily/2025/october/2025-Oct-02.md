# Daily Note - October 2, 2025

## Issues from Previous Notes

The core challenge is making Lyre syntax look and feel more musical. Current syntax with `sequence`, `parallel`, and `tone` is too verbose and implementation-focused rather than expressing musical ideas naturally. A potential solution is preprocessing code with comma expressions to expand into sequence forms, similar to Lisp's quote mechanism. Visual structure can be improved by splitting note sequences with `|` (treated as whitespace).

Logo color needs refinement - the purple "y" highlighting the reverse lambda is conceptually right but the specific shade needs work.
