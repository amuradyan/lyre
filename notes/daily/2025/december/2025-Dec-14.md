## On Lyre the lib and Lyre the course

I need a monorepo. I want Lyre to be extracted as a library that can be used independently of the course. The course then becomes a separate package that depends on Lyre the lib. This way, Lyre the lib can be versioned and released independently of the course content. The course can specify which version of Lyre it depends on, allowing for better management of updates and changes.

Thus I can enjoy working on Lyre the lib as a project, not something inside of an editor component.
