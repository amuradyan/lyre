## On additive synthesis and computation

As more harmonics are added, the superposition waveform transitions from smooth sine wave toward sawtooth shape. This explains why plucked strings sound bright - they contain many harmonics that sum to create the characteristic sharp-rise, gradual-fall sawtooth pattern. On the other hand, to make the slopes smooth, we need to add many more harmonics and that turns out to be computationally demanding.
