## On Interactive Playlist Implementation

Added an interactive playlist to the Index page that allows users to browse and play pre-written musical examples. The playlist features a collection of musical fixtures including classical pieces, popular songs, and educational examples that demonstrate different Lyre language capabilities.

Users can now easily discover and switch between examples like Pachelbel's Canon, Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, Still D.R.E., Beethoven's 5th Symphony, Seven Nation Army, and Für Elise. The interface presents featured examples prominently with additional examples available through a dropdown menu.
