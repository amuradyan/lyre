## On import issues with Lyre modules

The day started with an issue with Lyre being inaccessible through an import. This has been a recurring problem recently. Since the audio worklet has to live under `public` and I copy Lyre to `public`, but it actually lives under `src`, this causes issues I do not understand. This probably has a very specific reason and just needs to be investigated and fixed.

It turned out an old audio streaming component was using the outdated Lyre implementation and failing. After the imports were fixed, everything worked fine.
