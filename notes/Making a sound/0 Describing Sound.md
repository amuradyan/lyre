# Describing Sound

We are starting our journey to make the computer play music; step one is learning how to express sound as data.

Press a key on a piano and you hear a smooth, steady tone. How do we describe that to a computer? Think of tapping on a table: at a slow pace you hear separate taps; speed up enough and it blends into a continuous sound. Computers do the same by sampling - taking measurements at a given rate - often enough to be able to recreate the sound smoothly. The more samples we take, the smoother the sound will be.

By the end of this step, we’ll make the computer go “beep” for a specific duration. Along the way we'll also think of what happens when we tap faster or slower, figure out how to do volume, and learn that waves are mighty helpful when working with sound.

Before we do that, we’ll make sure we can give the computer clear, precise instructions.
